Pump VST3 for macOS

Quit your DAW before installing. Copy the complete Pump.vst3 bundle into:
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

For a development build, copy the complete pump-<build-label>.vst3 bundle.
Remove an older Pump bundle from this folder to avoid duplicate plugin IDs.
Restart your DAW and rescan VST3 plugins if needed.
Development builds are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized.
